Latest signed notes

Note 001 · 04.09.2026 · National · rev. 20.10.2026

The Third Objective

The Gang Suppression Force has an end-state. It was filed on 29 June and it names a recipient that has not existed in the period this desk studies.

The paragraph 24 framework names the institutions and the measures, so the end-state can now fail on capacity; the transition still goes to “the Government of Haiti”, which holds no electoral mandate.

J-004 · assessment · confidence moderate · signed 20.10.2026 · check 03.2027 · M7

Note 002 · 16.09.2026 · National · rev. 28.10.2026

La chaîne de garde — what happens to a person arrested by a foreign force

Resolution 2793 lets the Gang Suppression Force arrest. It tells the force to notify the Haitian police and to update the Security Council. It does not tell anyone to count what happens after that.

Until a GSF-involved arrest is counted at the point it reaches a Haitian judge, the third objective of Resolution 2793 has no instrument of measurement on the only holder that can prosecute.

J-002 · finding · confidence moderate · signed 16.09.2026 · check 03.2027 · M7
