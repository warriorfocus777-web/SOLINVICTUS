03 / Venezuela & Latin America

The Venezuela
Workout.

Work in progress

A restructuring thesis on Venezuela’s sovereign and PDVSA debt.

The project examines the claims, the potential drivers of recovery and the political and legal conditions surrounding a possible workout. It is the current focal point of the Latin America research agenda.

Questions guiding the paper +
  1. Who holds the claims, and how are their interests aligned?
  2. What assumptions would determine potential recoveries?
  3. What political and legal conditions could enable a restructuring?
