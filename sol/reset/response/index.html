Anatomy of a response

Follow one sudden-onset crisis through the IASC system, from the first hours to the exit. Then switch to “After Reset” to see what the Humanitarian Reset, launched by the Emergency Relief Coordinator on 10 March 2025, has changed at each step.

The scenario is generic. Times follow the IASC scale-up protocol (2018). Use the timeline dots, or the j and k keys, to move between steps.

Scope: the scale-up protocol covers sudden-onset crises and substantial deteriorations. It does not apply to refugee emergencies, which UNHCR leads under the Refugee Coordination Model.
