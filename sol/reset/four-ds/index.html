How to read this explorer

Keep reported facts, synthesis and interpretation distinct. Indicators may describe inputs, activities, outputs, reach or perceptions; they are not automatically evidence of outcomes or causality. The Stocktake itself says outcomes for people have not yet been assessed.

Evidence

Each figure is traceable to a named source, version and status.

Interpretation

Headlines and questions are labelled synthesis, not source quotations.

Uncertainty

Draft, partial-year and conflicting figures are shown rather than hidden.
