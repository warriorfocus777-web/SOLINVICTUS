01 · Casualty distribution

Not independently verified. One originator holds a quarterly count.

The UN (BINUH Human Rights Service) records at least 1,167 people killed and 459 injured between 1 July and 30 September 2026: 45% during gang attacks; 50% during operations by national security forces, including extrajudicial executions involving police officers, actions by a private military company, and GSF patrols; and 5% during violence by self-defense groups and unorganized members of the population (“Bwa Kalé”). S1p. 6

By place, the report gives 85% for the Port-au-Prince metropolitan area in its text and 85% for the West Department on its map, then 12% Artibonite, 2% Grand Nord, 1% Centre. See D-509-05. S1p. 6

S/2026/800 counts 1,123 killed and 440 injured between 15 July and 12 October 2026, 52% of them during security operations. It is the same originator, and its period is different: it is neither combined with the quarterly figure nor counted as corroboration. S2

Independent counts exist for one event only. For the attack on Kenscoff on the night of 23–24 August, the mayor of Kenscoff told AFP about 40 were killed; the UN counts at least 47 killed and 52 kidnapped; RNDDH counts at least 164 killed and more than 70 kidnapped across the July, late-August and mid-September attacks. The scopes differ; see D-509-09. S26 S1pp. 12–13 S28

Required to complete or challengeA second originator, independent of the UN, publishing a count of people killed and injured between 1 July and 30 September 2026, by actor or context. Independent counts exist for single events (Kenscoff), not for the quarter.

02 · GSF operational support and strength

Neither is independently established. Each figure rests on one originator.

Strength: 1,083 military and 16 civilian personnel on 8 July 2026 (UN). S3

About 1,500 troops deployed, according to the United States ambassador to the UN, 29 September 2026. One originator, relayed by two outlets. S11 S12

Ceiling: 5,500 uniformed personnel and 50 civilians. S8

On 1 September the GSF spokesperson said the force would not reach 5,500 by October and declined to give the number deployed, citing operational reasons. S30

Operations: starting in September, the GSF supported at least six operations by HNP specialised units (Croix-des-Bouquets, Kenscoff, Port-au-Prince and Tabarre; Dessalines), continued patrols in Cité Soleil and Tabarre, and conducted at least two independent operations (Port-au-Prince, Gros-Morne). Nine gang members were killed and two injured; about 20 people were arrested. S1pp. 20–21

The Human Rights Service monitored seven GSF operations during the quarter. See D-509-07. S1p. 32

804 GSF personnel (767 men, 37 women) attended human-rights sessions. This is a training count, not a strength figure. S1p. 32

Between 6 March and 31 July 2026 the force did not take part in operations against gangs in La Plaine du Cul-de-Sac and Cité Soleil. S4

03 · Written command clarity

Candidate: no. The parallel force on record answers to the Prime Minister's Office.

BINUH describes a government-established “task force” of police officers and personnel from a foreign private military company conducting ground and explosive-drone operations, and cites no instrument governing it. S1p. 19

Human Rights Watch records that the task force was created on 1 March 2025 by the Prime Minister and the Transitional Presidential Council, is coordinated by the Prime Minister's Office according to BINUH, operates with support from Vectus Global, and that its command structure has not been made public. S29

Two originators, one dated in the quarter, describe the force outside the GSF line of command. No written instrument places it under GSF command. The desk has not declared confidence; the judgment is unsigned.

El Salvador's transitional law (29 September, in force 2 October) concerns participation in the GSF, not a parallel contingent. S14

06 · PNH files reaching a judge / custody record

No record. No source held gives this number.

The HRS documented 44 cases of extrajudicial executions and attempted extrajudicial executions involving HNP personnel (37 deaths, 7 injuries). They will be referred to the General Inspectorate (IGPNH) in the fourth quarter of 2026. S1p. 30

07Method

  1. Two independent originators or empty.
  2. A republication of the same report is not corroboration; one originator through two channels counts once.
  3. Candidate: a cell that meets the originator test but has no declared confidence. It is shown, unsigned, until the desk signs or strikes it.
  4. Confidence declared before claim; never raised after.
  5. Sources graded A (official or primary), B (established press or monitor), C (not yet graded: listed, never counted).
  6. Every judgment marked signed or unsigned.
  7. Empty is a result. An empty cell is never filled by inference.
  8. Errors struck through, never deleted.
  9. Quarterly unit: 1 July–30 September 2026.
  10. No policy recommendations. No targeting lists. No operational advice.
  11. One question per note. Objects over narrative.
  • A Official or primary
  • B Established press or monitor
  • C Not yet graded — listed, never counted
