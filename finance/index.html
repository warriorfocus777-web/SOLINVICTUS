Two forces

The oldest shorthand in markets — and the colour code used across this site.

Bull ↑ +

A bull market is a sustained rise in prices. A common convention, though not a law, calls it a gain of 20% or more from a recent low. The name is usually traced to the way a bull attacks: horns first, upwards.

Bear ↓ −

A bear market is a sustained fall. By the same convention, a decline of 20% or more from a recent high. Bears are said to swipe downwards. Both terms describe what has already happened, not what will happen next.

On Solar Financial, green marks a rise and red marks a fall. Every change is also written with its sign and an arrow, so the meaning never depends on colour alone.
